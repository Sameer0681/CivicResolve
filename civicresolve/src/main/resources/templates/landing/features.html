Platform Features

Everything you need for civic action

A complete platform for citizens, officers, and administrators to manage civic complaints end-to-end.

Easy Complaint Filing

Multi-step form with category selection, location pinning, photo upload, and priority setting. File a complaint in under 2 minutes.

Real-time Status Tracking

Follow your complaint through every stage — Pending → Open → In Progress → Resolved — with full audit trail.

Auto Escalation

Complaints unresolved beyond the SLA are automatically escalated to senior officers, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.

Smart Notifications

In-app notifications keep all parties informed at every status change, comment, or escalation event.

Role-based Dashboards

Separate, purpose-built dashboards for Citizens, Officers, and Administrators — each with the right tools and data.

Analytics & Reports

Admins get powerful analytics on complaint volumes, resolution rates, department performance, and citizen satisfaction.

Location Intelligence

Pin exact issue locations on an interactive map. Officers can view geographic clusters of complaints for efficient routing.

Department Management

Admins can create and manage departments, assign officers, and configure SLA timelines per department.

Secure & Private

Spring Security-backed authentication with role-based access control. Your data is always protected.
