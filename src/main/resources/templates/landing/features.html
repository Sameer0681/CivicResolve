Capabilities

A connected municipal platform engineered for transparency and speed.

Feature 01

Report issues via voice dictation in Hindi or English, typed text, or by capturing direct photo evidence without tedious paperwork.

Instantly identifies the responsible municipal department across all 14 sectors, eliminating bureaucratic bouncing.

Workflow

7-Step Redressal Workflow

Complaint submission and a server-confirmed reference receipt.

STEP 01
Understand Problem

Citizens provide details through text or speech. Photo selection is a local preview and is not submitted.

STEP 02
Pinpoint Location

Users may enter a location, use browser location, or select a point on the map.

STEP 03
Department Routing

The selected category is sent with the complaint; department classification depends on the configured AI provider.

STEP 04
Dossier Generation

The server returns a reference ID after the complaint is saved.

STEP 05
Submission Receipt

A receipt confirms the saved complaint details; live status updates are not currently available.

STEP 06
Authority Contact Details

Municipal officer and department contact details are not configured.

STEP 07
Escalation Status

Automated SLA tracking and escalation are not configured in this deployment.

FAQ

Feature FAQs

Common questions regarding the AI classification and reporting capabilities.

How does the AI classify complaints into the 14 sectors?
The NLP engine evaluates semantic keywords in your typed or spoken text (e.g., words like “pothole” trigger Roads & Transport, while “streetlight” triggers Street Lighting) and suggests the appropriate authority. You retain full control to adjust the sector before submitting.
What happens during the photo upload scan?
Selected photographs can be previewed locally. They are not attached to the submitted complaint.
Are response-time updates available?
Response-time countdowns and automated escalation are not currently available.
