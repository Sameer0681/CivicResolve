Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reporting civic issues, location pinning, and automated escalation.

How does the 10-meter standing GPS location pinning work?
When you report an issue, CivicResolve requests high-precision device GPS geofencing via HTML5 Geolocation API. If permitted, it locks your coordinates with an accuracy radius within ±10 meters. In Step 2 of the wizard, you can also click anywhere on the interactive Leaflet map or drag the pin marker to specify the exact ground location.
Do I need to know which government department handles my complaint?
No! CivicResolve features automated problem classification. When you speak, upload a photo, or type problem keywords, the AI analyzes your problem text and automatically routes it to the corresponding authority among the 14 civic service sectors (e.g. PWD, Jal Nigam, DISCOM, Waste Management). You can also manually change the sector if desired.
What happens if a department does not resolve my issue within the SLA?
Every complaint carries a legally enforceable SLA (ranging from 12 to 72 hours depending on urgency and sector). If an issue breaches the SLA threshold, CivicResolve’s automated Escalation Engine kicks in, bumping the ticket up to Level 2 (Divisional Executive Engineer) and Level 3 (Municipal Commissioner) with automated breach alerts.
Can I submit photos or speak my grievance in Hindi?
Yes. The Multi-Step Wizard supports live voice dictation in English and Hindi using the browser's Web Speech API. You can also upload or snap direct photos of the problem (pothole, broken lamp, overflowing garbage), which the system scans and attaches as evidence.
How do I track the status of my complaint after filing?
Upon submission, you immediately receive a unique Master Docket ID (e.g., CR-2048). You can enter this ID on the homepage tracker or open your Citizen Dashboard anytime to see real-time field engineer progress, photos of completed work, and verification ratings.
Is CivicResolve free to use for all citizens?
CivicResolve is 100% free and open public civic infrastructure. There are no fees or hidden charges. It operates as an official gateway connecting citizens directly to state municipal redressal mechanisms.
