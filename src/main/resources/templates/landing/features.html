Nine Core Capabilities

A connected municipal platform engineered for transparency and speed.

Feature 01
Automated Problem Analysis

Understands citizen grievances using Natural Language Processing (NLP), semantic keyword extraction, and urgency detection.

Feature 02
Multi-Modal Reporting

Report issues via voice dictation in Hindi or English, typed text, or by capturing direct photo evidence without tedious paperwork.

Feature 03
10-Meter Standing GPS

Captures precise device standing coordinates and maps the issue directly to the corresponding municipal administrative ward.

Feature 04
Department & Sector Routing

Instantly identifies the responsible municipal department across all 14 sectors, eliminating bureaucratic bouncing.

Feature 05
Autonomous Dossier Generation

Compiles a structured complaint payload with unique tracking ID, timestamp, and enforceable SLA window.

Feature 06
Transparent Real-Time Tracking

Track complaint status through every stage: Submitted → Under Review → In Progress → Resolved.

Feature 07
Automated SLA Monitoring

Pushes reminders to designated ward officers and field engineers as SLA milestones approach, without requiring citizen calls.

Feature 08
Hierarchical Escalation Engine

Auto-escalates breached complaints upward to higher administrative officers when designated resolution windows elapse.

Feature 09
Official Service Mapping

Links grievances to official municipal departmental rosters to provide full officer contact information and public auditability.

7-Step Redressal Workflow

One complaint. One structured workflow. Accountable government response.

STEP 01
Understand Problem

Citizen provides input via voice, photo, or typed text. The system analyzes context, keywords, and problem type.

STEP 02
Detect Location

Standing GPS coordinates are acquired and bound to municipal ward boundaries to pinpoint jurisdiction.

STEP 03
Department Routing

Categorizes into one of 14 public service sectors and routes the file to the responsible municipal department.

STEP 04
Dossier Generation

A structured complaint payload is compiled with a unique tracking ID and assigned SLA clock.

STEP 05
Live Status Tracking

Both citizen and authority track real-time progression through the 4-phase lifecycle with timestamped logs.

STEP 06
Authority Contact Mapping

Provides the designated municipal officer designation, department helpline, and office location.

STEP 07 • THE ACCOUNTABILITY NET
Automated Hierarchical Escalation

If an assigned officer fails to review or act upon a complaint within the prescribed SLA window (e.g. 48h), the system triggers an automatic escalation event, promoting the file to higher administrative tiers.
