Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about submitting a complaint and following its progress.

What information do I need to file a complaint?
Provide a title, a description of the issue, its service sector, and a nearby location. Sign in with a citizen account to submit the form.
Do I need to know which department handles my complaint?
No. Choose the closest service sector from the list. CivicResolve assigns the department associated with that sector.
How can I follow up on a submitted issue?
Your receipt includes a reference ID. Use the Track page to see the complaint’s current status and service sector.
Can I submit a photo with my complaint?
This version accepts written complaint details only. Photo uploads are not available.
How do I track the status of my complaint after filing?
Enter the reference ID from your receipt on the Track a Complaint page.
Is CivicResolve free to use for all citizens?
Citizen registration and complaint filing are available without a fee. Service availability depends on the deployment.
